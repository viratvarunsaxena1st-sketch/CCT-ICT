aPASAT

Adaptive Paced Auditory Serial Addition Test — CCT

How this works

Digits from 1–9 are read aloud, one at a time. Add each new digit to the one spoken right before it — not a running total. A number flashes on screen after every pair: decide whether it's that sum.

  • It is the sum → press Left
  • It's the sum, off by one → press Right
  • Anything else → press nothing

Dichotic attention switching (experimental) — two voices now speak at once every interval: a low-pitched voice and a high-pitched voice. Only one is your target at a time. Add and judge digits from the target voice only, and let the other voice pass by unattended.

  • A short low tone → switch your attention to the low voice.
  • A short high tone → switch your attention to the high voice.
  • The target changes every few trials with no warning besides that tone, so listen for it.

The tone uses the beep volume setting below. Since two digits are read every interval, the fastest speed is also capped a bit higher than usual while this is on.
